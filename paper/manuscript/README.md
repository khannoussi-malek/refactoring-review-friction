# Manuscript — the methods paper

**Target venue: MSR, technical track.** Chosen because SEOSS 33, GHS and the
traceability literature this paper argues with are all MSR-community work, so the
critique reaches the audience that builds and uses linked-issue datasets. The
page limit also suits a two-table methods paper, which is the shape R4 forces.

**Target submission: Q4 2026 (October–December).** The author's target, not a
verified CFP deadline — check the call before relying on it. Phase D was budgeted
at roughly two weeks of part-time writing; six of the eight sections were
unwritten at scaffold time.

## The thesis

Every section, table and paragraph serves this one sentence. If it does not, it
belongs in the replication package, not the manuscript.

> **Architectural change is systematically absent from the records that empirical
> software engineering studies rely on** — measured across three independent
> record channels (commit messages, issue trackers, pull requests) in two
> ecosystems (Apache/Jira/Java and GitHub/TypeScript), with a taxonomy of six
> mechanisms by which a project silently fails to support an issue-linked study.

This reframes what `README.md` currently leads with. The commit-side versus
ticket-side traceability divergence remains the **strongest single
measurement**, but it becomes evidence for the broader claim rather than the
claim itself: a single-dataset critique is dismissible as an Apache artifact, and
a two-ecosystem, three-channel result is not.

`README.md` stays as the outward account and is not rewritten. The reframing
lives here.

## Rules that bind the drafting

**Every number must exist in `paper/numbers.md`** with a script path and a commit
hash (R7). If a number is not there, either derive it with a committed script and
add the provenance row, or do not use it. `PROVENANCE_CHECK.md` in this directory
is the audit.

**No claim may depend on manual coding by a single rater** (R6). The codebook's
25% precision and the TypeScript 30%/3% figures are reported **as findings about
those instruments**, never as instruments this paper's claims rest on. The one
exception is argued explicitly: `paper/matcher_validation.md` is a mechanical
check against a written rule, not a coded judgment.

**The six killed hypotheses appear once**, as a single motivating paragraph in
`intro.md` — *we set out to measure signal-to-action latency, six
operationalisations failed, and the sampling frame itself turned out to be the
finding* — and nowhere else. **They are not results of this paper.** Every named
cause in `README.md` §4 is preserved.

**1,822 never appears beside a per-project number.** Three project-count figures
travel together or not at all: 1,276 key-based, 2,506 name-union, 1,822 published
and not reproducible.

**No new analyses, corpora, RefactoringMiner runs or p-values** (R4). This ships
what exists.

**Held-out corpus.** Seven projects — Ozone, Tez, ZooKeeper, Ranger, Oozie, Knox,
Sqoop — contribute coverage and existence counts only. No timing statistic for
any of them may appear in the manuscript. HBase and Phoenix were dropped from the
held-out corpus on 2026-07-30 and quarantined in `spent/`; their coverage numbers
are still usable.
