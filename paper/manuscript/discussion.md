# 7. Discussion

**Status: to write last** (writing order 7). Draft after `results.md`.

## What must be here

**For researchers.** Project eligibility for issue-linked research cannot be
established from metadata. It requires reading commit messages from the project
itself, per project, before any outcome is measured — and if the outcome is
ticket-anchored, measuring the **ticket-side** rate, because a high commit-side
rate does not imply one. Report the bar, the attrition, and the rejected
candidates.

**For dataset builders.** The fields that decide usability are absent from the
standard frame. A traceability rate, tracker type and dominant reference channel
would be cheap to compute and would have saved this study 38 clones to keep 12.

**For the field.** If architectural change is invisible in three record channels
across two ecosystems, then a literature built on those records is measuring the
minority of architectural work that someone chose to expose. In the TypeScript
corpus that minority is measurably different from the rest: PR-routed
architectural commits are five times larger and four times more abstraction-heavy
than direct-push ones.

**What follows from the corpus limit, honestly.** At 12 projects the effective n
is capped at P/ICC whatever the corpus size — a ceiling of 600 at ICC 0.02
against the 769 the effect needs. The three ways out are in
`replication/CORPUS_FEASIBILITY.md`; option 1 (lower the bar, model the error) is
the one to avoid drifting into silently.

**Do not** propose the SATD-interval design here. That is a separate registered
report, gated on external judgment (`paper/SATD_NOVELTY.md`).
